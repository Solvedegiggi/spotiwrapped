How to Download Your Spotify Data

Follow this step-by-step guide to get your complete lifetime streaming history from Spotify.

1

1. Open Spotify Privacy Settings

Log into your Spotify account in your browser and visit the Account Privacy page.

Open Spotify Privacy Page
2

2. Request Extended Streaming History

Scroll down to the 'Download your data' section. Make sure to check the box for Extended streaming history. Then click Request data.

⚠️ Important: Do not select only 'Account data' (it only covers the last 1 year). Be sure to select Extended streaming history for your full lifetime history!
3

3. Confirm the Request via Email

Spotify will send a confirmation email with a link. Open it and click Confirm to start processing.

4

4. Wait for Spotify to Prepare Your Files

Spotify compiles your data over several days (usually between 2 to 5 days, up to 30 days). You'll receive an email as soon as it's ready.

5

5. Download & Drop It Here!

Download the ZIP file from the email. You can drag and drop the entire ZIP file directly onto the upload screen—no unzipping needed!
